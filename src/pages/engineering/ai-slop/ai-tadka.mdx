AI Tadka is a specific form of AI Slop: an unnecessary AI-generated "improvement" applied to functional engineering work, creating maintenance cost and organizational noise instead of value.

It can look like an inflated design document, boilerplate tests that prove nothing, or an AI-generated review that introduces requirements no one asked for.

The concept was introduced in [Introducing AI Tadka: When AI "Improvement" Makes Good Engineering Worse](/blogs/2026/07-26-introducing-the-word-ai-tadka).

This handbook chapter is being developed.
